About

Built Because It Needed to Exist.

Angrove gives serious questions a place that is private, lasting, and open to everyone. Saved personal data is encrypted on your device. It's a personal project by one person who believes this particular use of AI should exist.

Not a Chat Window, an Education Harness

From the Founder

I'm Ryan, a web designer and now a software engineer, with a lasting interest in philosophy, theology, and history. Angrove began when I learned that a capable language model could run entirely on a phone. That technical fact raised a more human question: what might someone finally be willing to ask if they knew the conversation could not leave their device?

I kept thinking about the questions people carry quietly, about faith, doubt, meaning, and truth, and about how little technology is built to help us stay with them. I wanted something that would make inquiry deeper rather than easier to avoid: a place to test an argument, follow a contradiction, and keep the ideas worth returning to.

I didn't build Angrove as a demonstration of what AI can do. I'm building it because I believe this particular use of it should exist, and because the people who need it most are often the least served by the technology we already have.

Most AI apps are built around a chat box. It works well for conversation, but conversation is a poor shape for learning something hard. It scrolls away, it agrees too easily, and it never shows you what you've actually understood.

Coding tools got this right early. They don't hand you a bare chat. They wrap the model in a harness built around the work itself, with the files, the tests and the workflow. Looking at them, I kept asking myself the same thing: what would a harness look like if it were built for education? What would I put around a model if the goal were for you to understand something, and not just to get an answer?

That question shaped everything in Angrove. Each feature exists because it serves understanding, and I keep asking whether a change helps you learn or only feels helpful. The features will keep changing as I learn what works. The question won't.

The result is a harness that interfaces the mathematical connection of ideas inside the language model to the user, so they can understand how ideas are interconnected.

To see the harness in action, walk through the User Guide, or read the product and engineering case studies for the architecture and tradeoffs, or follow the Development Notes for experiments and measured results.

What's next

Beyond the iPhone

Angrove is on the iPhone first, and a version fine-tuned for the app is on its way there too.

A desktop version for Mac is coming after that. It will ship with the fine-tuned model, but it won't be tied to it. You'll be able to decouple the model and plug in any local open-weight model you like, still running on your own machine, and you'll still get everything that makes Angrove what it is. The harness is the point, and the model is something you can swap.

The mission

Restoring the sanctity of thought.

Some of the most important questions a person can ask have nowhere safe to go. Questions about faith, meaning, and truth are too personal or too taboo to ask out loud, and the places we'd ask them now, the cloud and the feed, are built to harvest what we think.

AI is accentuating a crisis of meaning in many people, and some fear being optimized out of their future. I wanted to be on the side of AI that enhances our humanity instead of replacing it.

When I saw models small enough to run on a phone, I realized only a local model could offer true privacy. Angrove is the result: a private space for life's biggest questions, built with a moral responsibility to do the most good I can.

  • Not locked behind a subscription.
  • Not surveilled by a server.
  • Not filtered by an algorithm.
  • Yours. On your device. Always.
