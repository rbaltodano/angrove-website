Legal

Privacy Policy.

Effective October 8, 2026. This policy covers the Angrove iPhone app and this website, angrove.app, both made by Sine Viridian.

The short version. Angrove answers your questions on your iPhone. Your conversations, saved Insights, Study Topics and reading history stay on your device, encrypted. Angrove has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. Your information is not sold or shared. Data leaves your phone in only two situations: when Apple delivers the app’s model files, and when you choose to send a bug report.

What stays on your device

Angrove’s language model and source library run on your iPhone. Your questions and the model’s answers are never sent to a model server or anyone else in the current public beta. An optional Wikipedia search is in development, as described below.

Everything personal you create in Angrove is stored only on your device and encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is saved. This includes conversations and drafts, saved Insights and definitions, your Insight Tree, Study Topics, reading and discovery history, Home usage records, your name and custom instructions, and the app’s local backups. Apple’s Keychain protects the encryption keys; they are not synced to iCloud Keychain and are never sent off your device.

This content cannot be seen, recovered or restored. If the encryption key on your device is lost, the encrypted data cannot be read by anyone.

When data leaves your device

Model download from Apple. When you install Angrove, iOS downloads the app’s on-device model from Apple’s servers. Apple handles that download under the Apple Privacy Policy. The download does not include any of your conversations or saved content.

Bug reports you choose to send. If you use Report a Bug in the app (Settings → Report a Bug) or on this website, the submitted information includes what you type: a description, optional steps to reproduce, and an optional email address if you want a reply. Reports sent from the app also include the Angrove version and build number and your iOS version. Reports are delivered through Formspree, a form-processing service that forwards them to the support inbox; like any web service, it also receives your IP address when a report is sent. Nothing else from the app is attached, so please leave out private conversations or other sensitive details. Reports are used only to fix problems and reply to you, and are deleted when no longer needed. A report deletion request can be sent at any time.

Upcoming optional Wikipedia search. This feature is in development and is not available in the public beta yet. The planned behavior is to leave it off by default. If enabled, it would generate search keywords on your iPhone when the Library cannot support a question and send those keywords to Wikipedia’s public service to retrieve articles. It would not send the full question, conversation, or saved content. Like any web service, Wikipedia would receive the search keywords and your IP address under the Wikimedia Foundation privacy policy. This description covers the planned feature; the current public beta does not make these searches. Read the development note.

Things you share yourself. Export Conversations creates a readable, unencrypted file, and text you copy or share leaves Angrove through iOS. Where it goes from there is up to you.

iPhone features Angrove uses

  • Face ID. If you turn on App Lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. Angrove learns only whether the check succeeded; it never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications. The daily question and finished-answer alerts are scheduled on your device. Angrove has no push-notification server. Notification previews may show conversation titles on your Lock Screen.
  • Widget. The optional Question of the Day widget shows the day’s question on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Its text is stored encrypted with a separate key.
  • Read aloud. Speech is generated on your device. While a reading plays, iOS may show its title in Now Playing and on the Lock Screen.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone, the backup can include Angrove’s encrypted data. Apple manages those backups.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may provide crash reports and usage statistics to the app developer. These come from Apple, in a form Apple controls, and do not contain your Angrove content. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

This website

angrove.app uses no cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive standard request information such as your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy. The website’s bug report form is handled by Formspree as described above.

Your choices

  • Turn on App Lock, or export and import your conversations, in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Clear your Insight Tree in Settings, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.
  • Email bugreport@angrove.app to ask about, correct or delete a bug report you have sent.

Children

Angrove is not directed to children under 13. Personal information is not knowingly collected from children under 13. If a child has sent a bug report containing personal information, contact bugreport@angrove.app to request its deletion.

Changes to this policy

Changes to how Angrove handles information will be reflected on this page, along with an updated effective date, before taking effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: bugreport@angrove.app.
