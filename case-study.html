01 · The problem & my role

Give Inquiry Somewhere to Grow.

A conversation is easy to start and easy to lose. For questions about philosophy, theology, and history, I wanted a workspace where someone could test an argument, inspect its sources, and keep the concepts they wanted to return to.

I own the product design, SwiftUI implementation, local model integration, retrieval, persistence, and evaluation. Selected engineering work is explained here, and the app is available as a public beta on TestFlight. The full source is on GitHub.

My product hypothesis is that conversation, contextual definitions, and a lasting map of saved ideas can help people stay with difficult questions. That hypothesis still needs user validation; I’m not claiming a measured improvement in learning.

02 · The product

From a Question to an Insight.

The central flow starts with a question. A source-oriented answer leads to a contextual definition; the user chooses whether to save that definition as an Insight. Saved Insights become a semantic map that can be revisited in the Insight Tree and Study view.

  1. Ask

    Explore a question.

  2. Inspect

    Read the answer and its source.

  3. Save

    Keep a definition as an Insight.

  4. Connect

    Revisit related ideas in the map.

Automatic analysis can suggest broader Node Concepts. It does not silently save an Insight for the user. Semantic distance represents relatedness, not truth or importance.

03 · The architecture

A Whole System on the Phone.

Angrove uses stock instruction-tuned Gemma 4 E4B from the LiteRT Community package. It is not an Angrove fine-tune. Core ML MiniLM embeddings support local passage retrieval and relationships in the map.

Definitions, naming, and tree labels share the same runtime. A lifecycle manager controls loading, idle unloading, background transitions, and cancellation. There is no HTTP model backend or cloud fallback.

04 · Engineering decisions

The Tradeoffs Shape the Experience.

Retrieve evidence locally.

The bundled Library makes source passages available offline. Similar wording alone can retrieve the wrong evidence, so source routing and passage locators help the app find the text a question actually needs. The reader can inspect that text alongside the answer.

Make model work visible.

Answers, definitions, and tree tasks compete for the same engine. The queue gives users cancellation and reordering, while persisted conversation and branch identifiers keep late results attached to the original question after navigation. When local generation fails, the app exposes a failure rather than inventing a substitute answer.

Keep unsupported claims out of the answer.

Some source requests can return verified passages without another generation. Others use retrieved evidence or abstain when reliable evidence is absent. This makes sources inspectable, but it can also narrow coverage and produce unhelpful refusals. Retrieval quality needs its own evaluation.

Protect saved personal data.

Fully Encrypted saved personal data uses AES-256-GCM with Keychain-managed keys on your device. A separate widget key limits access, and startup validates ciphertext before migrating plaintext. Ongoing validation covers source integration, physical-device lock behavior, and backup recovery. Deliberate conversation exports are readable JSON; displayed content and screenshots are outside this storage boundary. Read the coverage and recovery details.

05 · Learning from the build

Let the Evidence Shape the Product.

The model is one part of the experience. I evaluate its answers alongside the sources it receives, the time someone waits, and the memory a real phone can spare. That work has shaped three decisions:

Improve the evidence behind the answer.

Recent model comparisons kept Gemma 4 E4B as the default and shifted the next work toward better retrieval. Optional Wikipedia search is in development, off by default, for questions the bundled Library cannot support. Read the model and source experiments.

Measure the wait through the whole app.

Phone testing separated model loading from answer generation and exposed the recurring cost of an engine restart. Shorter waits and showing answers as they are written remain engineering priorities. Read the iPhone performance note.

Make defaults earn their cost.

Native thinking roughly doubled answer time without improving aggregate objective passes across the two development sets tested. It stays off by default. These results guide a configuration choice; they are not a general accuracy claim. Read the thinking-mode comparison.

Earlier model and runtime probes also shaped the requirements for session retention and source use. That research is archived and was shelved in September 2026.

06 · What comes next

Validate the Product in People’s Hands.

The Angrove beta is open now on TestFlight. An App Store release is next. I have not published adoption, retention, or learning-outcome results. The next product questions are whether people revisit saved Insights, check the source passages, and find the map useful.

Ongoing engineering work focuses on phone lifecycle validation (backgrounding, memory warnings, and idle unloads), shorter waits around the engine restart, encryption migration and backup recovery, model delivery, and a supported-device matrix. Historical phone scores whose raw run artifacts were lost are not used as headline evidence here.
